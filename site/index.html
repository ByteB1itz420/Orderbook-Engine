UNDER THE HOOD

Small rules.
Exact behavior.

01

Price-time priority

Best price wins. Within one price, the first order in the FIFO queue fills first. No skipped order, no vague matching rule.

02

Two different replays

Synthetic incoming orders generate fills here. Historical feed events are observed book changes, not fresh commands to rematch.

03

Measured, not marketed

A clear baseline competes with a bounded compact alternative. Faster cancels did not mean faster adds or matches on the agent host.
